eSafe - Password Manager

Welcome to eSafe, your secure and user-friendly password manager! eSafe is designed to help you manage your passwords efficiently, generate strong passwords, and ensure your accounts are safe from breaches. With eSafe, you can store, generate, and manage your passwords with ease, all in one place.
Features

    Save Passwords: Store passwords for any account securely.

    Generate Strong Passwords: Create strong, randomized passwords with just one click.

    One-Click Copy: Easily copy passwords to your clipboard for quick use.

    Breach Check: Verify if your account or password has been compromised in a data breach.

    User-Friendly Interface: Simple and intuitive design for seamless navigation.

    Secure Storage: Your data is encrypted and stored safely.
